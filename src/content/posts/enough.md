There is one word I wish someone had taught me sooner. Enough.

We learn so many other words without trying. More. Next. Better. We know how to want. What we never learn is how to stop and say, this is enough, I am okay right here.

For most of my life, more was the goal. A little more money. A slightly bigger place. The next thing that was going to finally make me feel settled. And every time I got it, the feeling lasted about a week. Then the wanting moved somewhere else, and I followed it, like always.

It took me a long time to see what was really happening. More never arrives. It is not supposed to. The whole point of more is to keep you walking toward something you will never quite reach.

So one day I stopped walking.

I did not give up. I want to be clear about that, because it is easy to confuse the two. Giving up comes from being tired. Enough comes from finally seeing clearly. I stopped, not because I had failed, but because I looked around and realized I already had what I had been chasing the whole time.

Nobody can tell you where your enough is. It is not a number on a screen. You have to feel for it yourself. I found mine by asking quiet questions. What do I really need to feel safe? What do I keep buying that never makes me happier? When did I last feel like I had plenty?

The answers were smaller than I expected. Most of what I was chasing was never really mine. I had borrowed it from people I was comparing myself to, and from a phone full of lives I did not actually want.

When I finally believed I had enough, something in me went quiet. I stopped measuring my life against everyone else’s. I spent less, because I was not trying to fill a hole that was never there. I had more time, and slowly I started to enjoy the things that were already in front of me.

That is the gift of this small word. Enough does not make your life smaller. It makes it yours.

The world will never stop saying more. But you can be the one who finally says, I have enough.

And then you can put it down, and go live….!
