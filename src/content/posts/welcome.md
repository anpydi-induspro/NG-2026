Most of what matters in a day doesn't make it into the meeting notes. It's the pause before someone answers, the thought you have on the drive home, the question you keep circling back to.

This blog is a place for those things.

## What you'll find here

I'll write about work, about the ideas that shape how I live, and about the small moments that turn out to be bigger than they looked. Some posts will be short. Some will take a while to get to the point.

> The space between the lines is where the meaning usually hides.

Thanks for reading.
