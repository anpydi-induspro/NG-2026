I love animals. There is something about the way they give their whole heart to you, asking for nothing back, that makes life feel softer. I share my home with two Golden retrievers, and they have taught me more about being present than any book ever has By profession, I work in fintech, where finance meets technology. I help companies with their finance and tax work, and I enjoy finding better, smarter ways to solve old problems.

I am an immigrant. Building a new life in a new country was not easy. It came with hard days and proud ones, and it shaped the way I see the world. It made me patient. It made me strong.

But before all of that, I am a father. I have two beautiful daughters, and their laughter and endless questions fill my days with wonder.

These days, I am trying to put all of this into words. My family, my dogs, the road I have walked. I am still learning how to say it well. This is me, beginning to try.
